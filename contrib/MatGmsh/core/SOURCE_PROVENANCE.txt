Native MAT extraction, 23 November 2026

Source: MatGmsh/src at the local migration baseline.
Only raw dual construction, active branch classification/postfilters, topology
and optional local projection are retained. There is no parameterization,
quad meshing, old proximity/confidence filtering or standalone GUI/CLI here.

MatRaw.cpp retains the production seed/dual ordering and replaces libigl
winding by DiscreteCADWinding at circumcenters. The full solid-angle fallback
is double arithmetic, not an exact predicate.
matCircumcenter.cpp preserves the rational fallback and uses Gmsh's predicate
implementation; Gmsh owns predicate initialization.
MatFilter.cpp and the postfilter helpers initially preserve legacy decisions.
Legacy global-largest and closed-sheet policies remain explicitly callable
for behavior comparison; the pipeline chooses whether to apply them.

Dormant theta-HMA and legacy POS/CSV diagnostic blocks have been removed from
the mixed postfilter and classifier. Unsupported legacy modes fail explicitly;
the production automatic policy and mixed-wing removal remain unchanged.

A follow-up reachability audit removed the unused triangulated-topology utility,
post-projection vertex-merger wrapper, raw topology snapshot and its never-called
preserve-raw-topology rebuild mode. The active preserve-existing-branches BFS
is unchanged. Empty legacy barrier state and unused seed adjacency/model-name
fields were removed. General.NumThreads now bounds the native winding queries.
