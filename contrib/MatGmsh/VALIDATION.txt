================================================================

Stage 1: controlled old/new comparison
-------------------------------------
The original replay and native pipeline both reloaded the same cached surface
mesh. Plate, Doghouse, Sample2/3/4, SteppedFilletedBracket, FlangedPipeT,
DoubleShellCage and TwistedDuct produced exactly the same retained polygon
parents, triangle connectivity and branch labels. Coordinates agree to
floating-point rounding. After final cleanup, plate, Doghouse and TwistedDuct
were rerun; maximum coordinate difference was 3.18e-12. A two-plate test with
keep-components preserves two components and matches the original policy.
This establishes migration parity, not geometric correctness of the legacy
filter or convergence to a true medial axis.

Connected-front extension prototype, 23 November 2026 (evening)
-------------------------------------------------------------
This supersedes the Stage 3 implementation/results above. Raw MAT, filtering
and projection are unchanged: their coordinate/connectivity/provenance hashes
match the preceding performance audit on identical cached source meshes.

Short connected collars replace individually extrapolated long bands. Nearby
triangles are co-refined together with the native open arrangement API. Finite
contacts introduce selective characteristic-shadow cuts; coplanar competitors
are partitioned by equal normal advance. Rejected self-intersecting collars
are isolated spatially and the layer is retried, at most four times. Every
accepted layer retains the original MAT and previously accepted geometry.
The direction field has not been replaced by a new extrapolation method.

Isolated Release measurements, Apple M3 Max, one thread, one warmup plus three
measured repetitions. Same skin checkpoints/options as the previous audit:

Plate                  1013            131 / 131           0.211 / 0.191
TwistedDuct           23554            191 / 202          10.976 / 1.964
Doghouse              80553              0 / 1439         94.783 / 35.286

Totals are 131, 574 and 1479 original fronts respectively. Plate completes;
TwistedDuct and Doghouse remain partial. Different output coverage means these
are not timings for geometrically identical results. Initial-boundary length
fractions belonging to completed fronts are 100%, 33.7% and 93.3%; front counts
are not surface-area coverage percentages.

Doghouse accepts twelve layers, 42 local co-refinement calls and 8504 new
triangles. It retains one component. Forty initial fronts remain unresolved:
seven associated with local rejection, 33 with an interior free boundary at
the end of the layer budget. TwistedDuct accepts one layer and 1859 new
triangles; conflicts during the next layer still prevent further commitment.
A layer that fails leaves earlier validated layers in place.

All nine CTest groups pass. Extension has nine fixtures, open co-refinement
and its containment envelope have 24, strict intersection/containment has 14.
The new fixtures cover a finite contact split, its later completion, opposing
coplanar fronts, a finite cavity, exact-coordinate export, selective cutters,
coordinate-roundoff slivers, genuine protrusions, translations and subdivision.

Independent saved-output audits pass all three cases. CGAL reports zero
self-intersections and zero degenerate triangles. Every input MAT coordinate
is present bit-exact in each result: 573/573, 12022/12022, 40716/40716. Original
parent/branch sets agree; maximum relative parent-area change on Doghouse is
3.68e-13 (the other two are exactly equal in this audit). This independent
area check aggregates original Voronoi polygons, while construction also
checks each fixed triangle's descendants. All four run hashes agree per case.

Important numerical contract: exported binary64 contact coordinates cannot
always lie exactly on an oblique binary64 skin. The extension uses explicit
coordinate-roundoff containment, not a strict exact containment claim. Every
positive-area outside descendant must fit in the rounding neighborhood of ONE
common skin triangle; the radius is eight times the coordinate ULP norm. The
outside-area allowance is capped per source triangle, independent of refinement.
Coverage must retain descendants and satisfy its coordinate-perturbation area
bound. No thickness-scaled geometric tolerance substitutes for this check.
The stricter projection containment guard is unchanged. Diagnostic evidence
shows why ignoring zero-area Boolean descendants alone is insufficient, and
why collapsing Boolean cleanup is unsuitable for this preservation audit.

Native Gmsh application Plugin(MatGmsh), Stage=2, also passes the smoke check:
77/77 fronts complete, no error diagnostics, source before/after checkpoints
byte-identical. The native application, shared library and static test library
were rebuilt with the new implementation.

Limitations: incomplete industrial fronts, no unique-midsurface guarantee,

The one-second industrial target has not been achieved. The remaining conflicts
need local test cases and improved continuation/rounding behavior.

Input-quality correction following visual review
------------------------------------------------
Doghouse still contains residual wings in the filtered MAT supplied to the
extension. Since this prototype freezes every input face, those wings are
retained and their free edges can also be continued. The 1439/1479 result
measures termination of the supplied fronts; it does not validate wing removal
or midsurface quality. The unchanged filter removed 157 zones (2505 Voronoi
polygons), but does not measure remaining false positives. The branch classifier
uses aggregate features, and the mixed post-filter only revisits retained
branches touching a free edge of the raw MAT. Local visual branch/zone diagnosis
is needed before attributing particular surviving wings to a specific rule.

Tetrahedron-based raw MAT and collapse filter, 25 November 2026
---------------------------------------------------------------
Branch feature/matgmsh-core-b. The C++ follows the Python prototype validated
on the synthetic corpus (topology exact on 199 of 200 cases); the comparison
of the C++ with that prototype on the same cached skins has NOT been run yet.

Unit tests (mat_native_skin_sites, mat_native_tet_interior,
mat_native_collapse): sites restricted to the skin triangles; certificates of
a cube, of a flipped, an open and a pinched surface; interior, exterior, flat
and on-skin tetrahedra, with one and three threads; dual of one edge; plate,
L bracket, holed plate and hollow box certified with the expected Euler
characteristics; burn time of an inner polygon; elementary collapses of small
complexes; convex edges of a cube; plate reduced to its mid-plane, T junction
keeping its triple line, hollow box with convex outer and concave inner edges.

Two rules differ from the prototype, on skins with several shells only: a
tetrahedron is interior when the winding number is odd (the prototype tested
nonzero, which fills a cavity whose shell is oriented like the outer one),
and the material side of each shell comes from the interior tetrahedra (the
prototype identified no convex edge on such skins).
