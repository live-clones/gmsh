The fonts Gmsh draws its strings with when it has no window (pictures made in
batch mode, through the API or by builds without the graphical interface):
Gmsh Sans, Gmsh Serif and Gmsh Mono, in regular, bold, italic and bold italic,
which are subsets of the Liberation fonts 2.1.5 (Latin-1, Greek and a few
mathematical signs, without hinting), renamed as the SIL Open Font License
asks of modified versions. They are metric-compatible with Helvetica, Times
and Courier, the fonts Gmsh names in its options and in its vector output.

fonts.h is generated by make_fonts.py (see there). The fonts are licensed
under the SIL Open Font License 1.1: see OFL.txt.
